You can remove the data {{ product }} holds about you or your social accounts in any of these ways:

1. Disconnect a social account

In the Service, open Channels, choose the account, and select Disconnect. Its access token is deleted immediately, along with the analytics, comments, and messages copied for that account.

2. Remove access from the platform

  • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook Settings › Security and login / Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites) › remove {{ product }}.
  • YouTube / Google: Google security settings › remove {{ product }}.
  • TikTok: TikTok app › Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services permissions › remove {{ product }}.

Once access is revoked the Service can no longer read or publish to that account, and its stored data for that account is deleted.

3. Delete your user account

In the Service, go to Settings › Profile › Delete account. Your personal data is permanently deleted.

4. Ask us

Email {{ email }} from the address on your account, or name the social account concerned. We confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.
