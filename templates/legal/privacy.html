{{ product }} ("the Service") is a social media management tool operated by {{ entity }} ("we", "us"). It lets our team and our clients plan, publish, and monitor content on social media accounts they own or manage. This policy explains what data the Service handles and how.

Data we collect

  • Account data: name, email address, password hash, and profile photo of people who sign in to the Service.
  • Connected social accounts: when you connect an account on Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Google Business Profile, or another supported platform, we receive the access tokens and the profile information that platform shares with the permissions you grant (for example account name, ID, and avatar).
  • Content: posts, captions, images, and videos you create or upload in the Service, and the publishing status of each post.
  • Engagement and analytics: comments, mentions, direct messages, and performance metrics (such as views, reach, and likes) that the connected platforms make available for your accounts.
  • Technical data: server logs (IP address, browser, timestamps) used for security and troubleshooting.

How we use data

We use this data only to provide the Service to you: publishing and scheduling your content, showing your inbox and analytics, enabling replies and approvals, and keeping the Service secure. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use data received from platform APIs for advertising or to build profiles of people.

Platform data

  • Access tokens are stored encrypted and are used only to perform the actions you request.
  • YouTube: the Service uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the Service's access at any time at Google security settings.
  • The Service's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) and TikTok: data is used according to each platform's developer terms. You can remove the Service's access from the platform's own settings (for Facebook: Settings › Business Integrations / Apps and Websites).

Sharing

We share data only with the social platforms you connect (to carry out your requests) and with infrastructure providers that host the Service on our behalf. We may disclose data if required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep data while your account or the connected social account is active. When you disconnect a social account, its tokens are deleted immediately and its copied analytics and messages are removed. You can request deletion of all your data at any time; see Data Deletion.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS, tokens are encrypted at rest, and access is limited to authorized team members.
